Here you'll find my poster from the [SKAO Science Meeting 2025](https://www.skao.int/en/science-users/skao-science-meeting-2025) held in Görlitz, Germany. It highlights how the forthcoming Square Kilometre Array telescope will open new windows of discovery for exoplanets and brown/ultracool dwarfs. The full findings will soon appear in the publication [Advancing Astrophysics II](https://www.skao.int/en/science-users/557/advancing-astrophysics-ii).
